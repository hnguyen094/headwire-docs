Get Headwire Helper

Last updated 2026-10-29

Headwire Helper is an optional, free app for your Mac. With it, Headwire on your iPad or iPhone shows your Mac’s screen over a USB cable or wirelessly, without a capture card, and controls it without Bluetooth. Install it on the Mac you want to see, not on your iPad or iPhone.

Install it on your Mac

On your Mac, open the App Store and search for Headwire Helper.

There is also a Direct download of Headwire Helper, which isn’t on the Mac App Store. It adds what Apple doesn’t allow in the Mac App Store, such as letting your iPad or iPhone extend your Mac as a second display. Headwire says when a feature needs it. Open the downloaded file, then move Headwire Helper to your Applications folder.

Headwire Helper needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 27 or later.

Set it up

  1. Open Headwire Helper. Its icon is in the menu bar at the top of the screen.
  2. Turn on Streaming to show your Mac’s screen, and Input to control your Mac. macOS asks for Screen Recording and Accessibility; allow each. Headwire Helper listens for your iPad or iPhone only while Streaming or Input is on.
  3. In the Headwire Helper menu, choose Pair a Device. It shows a code.
  4. On your iPad or iPhone, open Headwire, then Settings › Connection › Pair with your Mac, choose your Mac, and type the code. Or scan the code with the Camera app.

The first time Headwire looks for your Mac, iOS asks whether it may find devices on your local network. Choose Allow to connect over Wi-Fi or Ethernet.
