Get Headwire Connect

Last updated 2026-11-05

Headwire Connect is the Mac app for Headwire. With it, Headwire on your iPad or iPhone shows your Mac screen and plays its sound over a USB cable or wirelessly, and controls the Mac with the most features Headwire has. Install it on the Mac you want to see, not on your iPad or iPhone.

It is one of two ways to use Headwire. The other, a capture card and Bluetooth, needs nothing installed on the Mac; Headwire Support has its steps. Use either, or both.

Install it on your Mac

Headwire Connect from the Headwire website

It adds what Apple doesn’t allow in the Mac App Store, such as letting your iPad or iPhone extend your Mac as a second display. Headwire says when a feature needs it.

Download Headwire Connect A zip file from this website’s releases on GitHub.

On your Mac, open the downloaded file, then move Headwire Connect to your Applications folder. If an earlier copy under its old file name, HeadwireHelper, is there, move it to the Trash.

Headwire Connect from the App Store

Headwire Connect is on its way to the Mac App Store. Once it’s there, on your Mac, open the App Store and search for Headwire Connect.

What it needs

A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 27 or later. To stream, Headwire Connect from the App Store also needs a display connected to the Mac, such as a monitor, the capture card or an HDMI dummy plug.

Set it up

  1. On your Mac, open Headwire Connect. Its icon is in the menu bar at the top of the screen.
  2. On your Mac, turn on Streaming in the Headwire Connect menu to show your Mac screen, and Input to control your Mac. macOS asks for Screen Recording and Accessibility; allow each. Headwire Connect listens for your iPad or iPhone only while Streaming or Input is on.
  3. On your Mac, choose Pair a Device in the Headwire Connect menu. It shows a code.
  4. On your iPad or iPhone, scan the code with the Camera app. Or open Headwire, then Settings › Add Mac, choose your Mac, and type the numbers.

The first time Headwire looks for your Mac, iOS asks whether it may find devices on your local network. Choose Allow to connect over Wi-Fi or Ethernet.
