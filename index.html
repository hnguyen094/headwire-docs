For iPad and iPhone

Your Mac, on your iPad.

Headwire shows the Mac in the room full screen on your iPad or iPhone, plays its sound, and lets you work it with touch, a trackpad, a mouse or a keyboard. It’s made for a Mac in the same room, like a Mac mini without a monitor.

Get Headwire Two ways to connect

Two ways to connect

Plug in a capture card, or install Headwire Connect.

Both show your Mac screen and play its sound, and both let your iPad or iPhone drive the Mac. Pick the one that fits your desk.

A capture card and Bluetooth

Nothing to install on the Mac.

  • Plug a USB capture card into your iPad and connect it to the HDMI port on your Mac. Headwire shows the picture edge to edge, at the resolutions and frame rates your capture card offers.
  • The picture comes straight from the HDMI port, with no app running on the Mac, so it shows the login window too.
  • Over Bluetooth, your iPad or iPhone becomes a keyboard and mouse for your Mac. Pair it once in System Settings on the Mac.

How to set it up

Headwire Connect

By cable or wirelessly, with the most features.

  • Install Headwire Connect on your Mac, and it sends your Mac screen and sound to your iPad or iPhone over a USB cable, your network, or peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. No capture card needed.
  • Input through it adds trackpad-style scrolling and typing any character, whatever the keyboard layout.
  • Copy text on one and paste it on the other, and send files both ways.

Get Headwire Connect

Use either, or both. Headwire picks the connection, and moves to another when one drops.

Made for your hands

Work your Mac the way you hold your iPad.

  1. Tap, or use it like a trackpad

    Tap where you want to click, or move the pointer as on a trackpad. Right-click with a two-finger tap or a long press. Drag, scroll, and pinch to zoom. Swipe with three or four fingers for Mission Control, App Exposé and Spaces.

  2. Bring your keyboard

    Type on a hardware keyboard or the onscreen keyboard, or dictate. A trackpad or mouse connected to your iPad works on the Mac too, and Apple Pencil points and clicks where it touches.

  3. Made to fit your screen

    Fill the screen, see the whole picture, or zoom in on what matters. Turn on a row of media, brightness and Mission Control keys when you want them.

  4. Hear your Mac

    Sound plays from the capture card’s HDMI audio or from Headwire Connect, and you can nudge its timing to match the picture.

On iPhone too

Your Mac in your pocket.

On iPhone, Headwire shows your Mac through Headwire Connect, and controls it through Headwire Connect or over Bluetooth.

Private by design

Your Mac stays between your devices.

  • Video from a capture card never leaves your iPad.
  • Headwire Connect streams only to devices you’ve paired, over a cable, your local network or peer-to-peer Wi-Fi, never the internet, and the connection is encrypted.
  • No accounts, no advertising and no tracking. Usage data is off until you turn it on.

What you need

A Mac, and an iPad or iPhone.

To see your Mac on iPad
A USB capture card and an HDMI cable, or Headwire Connect on the Mac. A capture card needs an iPad with USB-C.
To see your Mac on iPhone
Headwire Connect on the Mac.
To control your Mac
Bluetooth on the Mac, or Headwire Connect.
For Headwire Connect
A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 27 or later. Headwire Connect from the App Store also needs a display connected to the Mac to stream, such as a monitor, the capture card or an HDMI dummy plug.
For Headwire
iPadOS 26 or iOS 26 or later.

Video that its app protects, as many streaming services do, shows as black through a capture card. The rest of the screen still works.
