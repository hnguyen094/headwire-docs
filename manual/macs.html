Pairing and more than one Mac

Pair your iPad or iPhone with Headwire Connect on your Mac once, and Headwire connects to it by itself from then on. Pair more than one Mac and switch between them.

Pairing is for Headwire Connect. To use your Mac with a capture card and Bluetooth instead, with nothing installed on it, see Capture card and Bluetooth.

Add a Mac

In Headwire, open Settings and tap Add Mac… under your Macs. Add Mac lists every Mac with Headwire Connect it can find under Macs nearby, each once, with how a tap on it pairs.

A short video of this is on its way.

While the list is empty, Headwire is still looking. On your Mac, turn on Streaming or Input in the Headwire Connect menu: Headwire Connect listens only while one of them is on.

Each Mac’s row says how it pairs:

The row saysWhat happens when you tap it
Connected by USB cableHeadwire asks you to confirm the Mac, then pairs over the cable with nothing to type. On your Mac, click Allow if it asks.
Shown on the capture cardHeadwire asks Headwire Connect to show its pairing code on your Mac’s screen, then reads it through the capture card, so there’s nothing to type.
On this networkHeadwire asks for the code your Mac shows.

A Mac you’ve paired already is marked Paired and can’t be chosen again.

Pair with a code

  1. On your Mac, choose Pair a Device… in the Headwire Connect menu. It shows a code.
  2. In Headwire, tap your Mac under Macs nearby.
  3. Type the 12 numbers in Pairing code. Spaces and hyphens don’t matter. Tap Pair.

If the code is wrong, Headwire says so and you can type it again. To pair with a different Mac, tap Choose another Mac.

Pair through the capture card

While the capture card shows your Mac’s screen, Headwire can read the code itself. Tap the Mac; Headwire asks Headwire Connect for its code, finds it on the screen and pairs. If the code doesn’t appear, on your Mac, choose Pair a Device in the Headwire Connect menu, or tap Type the code instead.

Pair by scanning the code

The code Headwire Connect shows is also a link. On your iPad or iPhone, scan it with the Camera app and tap the link: Headwire opens and asks Pair with this Mac?. It shows the Mac’s key; pair only if it matches the one in Headwire Connect’s pairing window on your Mac.

Pair over the USB cable

With your Mac and your iPad or iPhone connected by a USB cable, Headwire offers the Mac on the cable by itself. Tap Pair to confirm it, or Cancel. On your Mac, click Allow if Headwire Connect asks.

Once paired, Headwire says it’s paired with your Mac and connects to it by itself from now on. Tap Done.

Other ways in

Under Other ways, Connect over Bluetooth… sets up this iPad or iPhone as your Mac’s Bluetooth keyboard and mouse, with nothing installed on your Mac (see Capture card and Bluetooth). Get Headwire Connect on your Mac sends the link to Headwire Connect to your Mac, for example by AirDrop.

More than one Mac

Pair each Mac the same way. Headwire shows one Mac at a time, and remembers what each Mac taught it, such as its keyboard layout and how it last connected, so switching never carries one Mac’s setup to another.

A short video of this is on its way.

Switch Macs

  • While more than one Mac is paired, a Macs menu sits beside Settings over the stream. It lists each Mac under Mac on screen, with the one on screen checked. Choose another to show it.
  • With a hardware keyboard, press Control-Option-Command with 1 to 9 to show the Mac at that place in the list, in the order you paired them, or with 0 for the overview.
  • In a Mac’s page in Settings, tap Show on screen.

Headwire lets go of every key and click on the old Mac before it shows the new one.

See every Mac at once

Choose All Macs in the Macs menu to open Your Macs: a live tile for every paired Mac, each saying whether it’s on screen, awake, asleep, at the login window or can’t be reached. Tap a tile to show that Mac, or tap Done.

Each Mac’s page in Settings

Each paired Mac has a row at the top of Settings. Tap it to open its page.

A short video of this is on its way.
  • Status says how the Mac’s Picture arrives and how its Controls reach it.
  • Show on screen puts this Mac on screen.
  • Mac display chooses Mirror main display or Extend as a second display. Extending needs the picture from Headwire Connect, and Headwire Connect from the Headwire website.
  • Play sound on chooses where this Mac’s sound plays.
  • The Picture, Connection and Controls choices (see How your iPad or iPhone connects) show in Advanced mode, or while one isn’t Automatic.

The rest of the page is in Settings.

Forget a Mac

On the Mac’s page, tap Forget this Mac and confirm. Headwire stops connecting to it and forgets its settings. To remove your iPad or iPhone on the Mac’s side too, on your Mac, open the Headwire Connect menu, choose it under Devices and forget it there.
