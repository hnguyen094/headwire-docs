Pairing and more than one Mac

Pair each Mac with Headwire Connect once, then switch between them.

Pair a Mac

A short video of this is on its way.

In Settings › Add Mac…, each Mac’s row says how it pairs:

The row saysThen
Connected by USB cableTap Pair. No code.
Shown on the capture cardHeadwire reads the code itself.
On this networkType the code your Mac shows.

Or scan the code on your Mac with the Camera app. Set up Headwire Connect.

Switch Macs

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. Tap Macs.
  2. Choose a Mac.

Or press Control-Option-Command with 1 to 9. All Macs, or Control-Option-Command-0, shows every Mac at once.

Forget a Mac

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. On the Mac’s page in Settings, tap Forget this Mac, then Forget.
  2. On your Mac, in the Headwire Connect menu, choose your iPad, then Forget name…
