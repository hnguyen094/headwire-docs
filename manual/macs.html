Pairing and more than one Mac

Pair your iPad or iPhone with Headwire Connect on your Mac once, and Headwire connects to it by itself from then on. Pair more than one Mac and switch between them.

Pairing is for Headwire Connect. To use your Mac with nothing installed on it, see Capture card and Bluetooth.

Add a Mac

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. In Headwire, open Settings.
  2. Under your Macs, tap Add Mac…. Every Mac with Headwire Connect that Headwire finds is listed under Macs nearby.
  3. Tap your Mac. Its row says how it pairs:
The row saysWhat happens when you tap it
Connected by USB cableHeadwire asks you to confirm the Mac, then pairs over the cable with nothing to type (Pair over the USB cable).
Shown on the capture cardHeadwire reads the pairing code off your Mac’s screen through the capture card, so there’s nothing to type (Pair through the capture card).
On this networkHeadwire asks for the code your Mac shows (Pair with a code).

A Mac you’ve paired already is marked Paired. While the list is empty, Headwire is still looking: on your Mac, turn on Streaming or Input in the Headwire Connect menu.

Pair with a code

  1. On your Mac, choose Pair a Device… in the Headwire Connect menu. It shows a code.
  2. In Headwire, tap your Mac under Macs nearby.
  3. Type the 12 numbers in Pairing code. Spaces and hyphens don’t matter. Headwire pairs once the last one is in.

If the code is wrong, Headwire says so and you can type it again (Pairing with Headwire Connect). To pair with a different Mac, tap Choose another Mac.

Pair through the capture card

While the capture card shows your Mac’s screen, Headwire reads the code itself.

  1. In Add Mac, tap the Mac marked Shown on the capture card.
  2. Wait while Headwire Connect shows its code and Headwire reads it. Headwire pairs by itself.

If the code doesn’t appear, tap Type the code instead and pair with a code.

Pair by scanning the code

  1. On your Mac, choose Pair a Device… in the Headwire Connect menu.
  2. On your iPad or iPhone, scan the code with the Camera app.
  3. Tap the link. Headwire opens and asks Pair with this Mac?
  4. On your Mac, click Show Key in the pairing window.
  5. Check that it matches the key Headwire shows.
  6. In Headwire, tap Pair.

Pair over the USB cable

  1. Connect your Mac and your iPad or iPhone with a USB cable.
  2. In Headwire, open Settings › Add Mac….
  3. Tap the Mac marked Connected by USB cable.
  4. Tap Pair.
  5. On your Mac, click Allow if Headwire Connect asks.

Other ways in

Under Other ways in Add Mac:

  • Connect over Bluetooth… makes this iPad or iPhone your Mac’s Bluetooth keyboard and mouse (Capture card and Bluetooth).
  • Get Headwire Connect on your Mac sends the link to Headwire Connect to your Mac, for example by AirDrop.

More than one Mac

Pair each Mac the same way. Headwire shows one Mac at a time and remembers each Mac’s own setup, such as its keyboard layout and how it last connected.

A short video of this is on its way.

Switch Macs

  • Macs menu: while more than one Mac is paired, a Macs menu sits next to Capture over the stream. Choose a Mac under Mac on screen.
  • Keyboard: press Control-Option-Command with 1 to 9 for the Mac at that place, in the order you paired them, or with 0 for every Mac at once.
  • Settings: on a Mac’s page, tap Show on screen.

Headwire lets go of every key and click on the old Mac before it shows the new one.

See every Mac at once

Choose All Macs in the Macs menu to open Your Macs: a live tile for every paired Mac, saying whether it’s on screen, awake, asleep, at the login window or can’t be reached. Tap a tile to show that Mac.

Change one Mac’s settings

In Settings, tap the Mac’s row at the top. Its page puts the Mac on screen, mirrors or extends its display, picks where its sound plays, and holds its connection choices (A Mac’s page covers every row).

A short video of this is on its way.

Forget a Mac

  1. On the Mac’s page, tap Forget this Mac.
  2. Confirm. Headwire stops connecting to it and forgets its settings.

To remove this iPad or iPhone on the Mac’s side too:

  1. On your Mac, open the Headwire Connect menu.
  2. Under Devices, point at this iPad or iPhone.
  3. Choose Forget name…, where name is this iPad’s or iPhone’s.
