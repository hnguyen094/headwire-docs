Capture card profiles

What Headwire learns about each capture card, and how to correct it.

Open a profile

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. In Settings › General, turn on Advanced mode.
  2. In Settings › Connections, tap your capture card under Capture cards.

The one in use also opens from its format page: Capture card profile.

Correct the picture

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. Brightness range: if blacks look grey or crushed, set Read as to Video (16–235) or Full (0–255).
  2. Colour: if reds look orange, set Read as to HD (BT.709) or SD (BT.601).

Headwire picks both by itself; change them only if the picture looks wrong.

Calibrate a capture card again

A short video of this is on its way.

With Headwire Connect, tap Calibrate now in the profile: Headwire flashes a test pattern on your Mac and measures the capture card. With only a capture card, it doesn’t show.

Forget a capture card

A short video of this is on its way.

At the end of the profile, tap Forget capture card.
