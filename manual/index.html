The Headwire manual

Headwire puts your Mac’s screen and sound on your iPad or iPhone, and drives the Mac with touch, a trackpad, a mouse or a keyboard. This manual shows how to get going and where to find every setting.

Two ways in

Headwire reaches your Mac in two ways. Use one, or both: with both, Headwire picks the connection that works best and moves to another when one drops.

A short video of this is on its way.

A capture card and Bluetooth

Nothing to install on the Mac: a capture card brings its picture and sound to an iPad, and Headwire is the Mac’s Bluetooth keyboard and mouse.

  1. Connect your Mac’s HDMI port to the capture card.
  2. Plug the capture card into your iPad.
  3. Open Headwire.
  4. Allow Camera and Microphone when asked. Your Mac’s screen appears.
  5. On your Mac, open System Settings › Bluetooth, then click Connect beside Headwire.
  6. Confirm the code on your Mac, then tap Pair on your iPad.

Set up a capture card and Bluetooth has every step.

Headwire Connect

An app on your Mac sends its screen and sound to an iPad or an iPhone by USB cable or wirelessly, with the most features, such as typing any character, the clipboard and files.

  1. On your Mac, install Headwire Connect from Get Headwire Connect.
  2. On your Mac, in the Welcome window, click Turn On beside Streaming, then allow what macOS asks.
  3. Click Turn On beside Input, then allow what macOS asks.
  4. In Headwire, open Settings › Add Mac….
  5. Tap your Mac.
  6. If Headwire asks for a code, on your Mac, click Show Pairing Code in the Welcome window.
  7. In Headwire, type the 12 numbers. Your Mac’s screen appears.

Set up Headwire Connect has every step.

What you need

Headwire runs on iPadOS 26 and iOS 26 or later.

A short video of this is on its way.
  • For a capture card: an iPad with a USB-C port, and a USB video capture card, the common “HDMI to USB” kind, with a cable from an HDMI port on your Mac.
  • For Headwire Connect: Headwire Connect on your Mac, and a USB cable or the same network for both. Get Headwire Connect has the download and the steps on the Mac.
