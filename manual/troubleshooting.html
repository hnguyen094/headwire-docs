When there’s no message

A short video of this is on its way.

Black bars around the picture

Set Scaling to Fill or Zoom to content.

Text looks soft

Set Optimize for to Sharpness, or pick a larger capture card resolution.

Motion looks choppy, or the pointer lags

Use a USB cable, and set Optimize for to Responsiveness.

Blacks look grey, or colours look off

Set Read as in the capture card’s profile.

Sound and picture out of step

Move Sound timing Earlier or Later. With Headwire Connect, turn on Advanced mode first.

No sound, and no message

Check Play Mac sound on isn’t Mac, and this iPad’s or iPhone’s volume.

A key stays held down

Tap Release all keys in Keyboard.

Command-Tab doesn’t reach your Mac

Press Option-Tab. More shortcuts.

Keys type the wrong characters

Set Mac keyboard layout to your Mac’s.

Clicks and typing do nothing

Set up Bluetooth, or turn on Input in the Headwire Connect menu.

Touch does nothing in a window

Make Headwire full screen, or use Apple Pencil, a mouse or a trackpad.

Your Mac isn’t in Add Mac

On your Mac, turn on Streaming or Input. Use a USB cable or the same network.

Headwire isn’t in your Mac’s Bluetooth list

In Headwire, tap Connect over Bluetooth… under Add Mac… or on your Mac’s page. On your Mac, look again in System Settings › Bluetooth.

Wake your Mac.
Connecting to your Mac…
Wait; the picture follows.
Starting capture…
The capture card is slow to start. If it stays, plug it in again. If it can’t draw, Headwire names why: no picture, paused or stopped.

Wake that Mac, or switch Macs.
Capture paused
Close other apps on screen or using the capture card. It resumes by itself.
Capture card unplugged
Plug it back in.
Capture stopped, Capture card not ready
Tap Try again, or plug it in again.
Capture card not supported
Try another capture card, or Headwire Connect.

On your Mac, turn on Streaming.
Turn on Screen Recording
On your Mac, turn on Headwire Connect in Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording.
Reopen Headwire Connect

Your Mac isn’t sending a picture, No picture from the Mac
Tap Try again. If it stays, reopen Headwire Connect.
Your Mac stopped sharing its screen, The display went away, The Mac stream stopped
It retries. If it stays, reopen Headwire Connect.
Your Mac’s video encoder stopped
On your Mac, choose Try Again in the Headwire Connect menu.
Your Mac has no display
Plug in a display or the capture card, or use Headwire Connect from the Headwire website.

Your Mac shows its own display instead of extending

A short video of this is on its way.

The line under Mac display says which to do:

  • Install Headwire Connect from the Headwire website.
  • On your Mac, turn on Let devices extend this Mac.
  • Turn this iPad or iPhone to another shape.
  • Disconnect the other device.

Update Headwire

Can’t read Headwire’s keys
Unlock this iPad or iPhone, then tap Try again.
Local Network access is off
Tap Open Settings and turn on Local Network.

Pairing

A short video of this is on its way.

Under Couldn't pair, the line says what happened:

  • The code didn’t match, or looks mistyped: check it against your Mac.
  • Too many tries: wait, then Try again.
  • Pairing isn’t open, timed out or stopped: on your Mac, choose Pair a Device….
  • Your Mac is locked: unlock it.
  • The code wasn’t read through the capture card: type it.
  • Anything else: Pair again with a new code.
