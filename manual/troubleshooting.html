When there’s no message

Some problems show nothing on screen. Find yours by what you see.

A short video of this is on its way.

There are black bars around the picture

Your Mac’s screen and this screen are different shapes. In Settings › Picture, set Scaling to Fill or Zoom to content (How the picture fits).

Text looks soft

The picture is smaller than this screen.

  • With Headwire Connect: set Optimize for to Sharpness in Settings › Picture.
  • With a capture card: choose a larger resolution on the capture card’s Format page, in Advanced mode.
  • Either way: Sharpen picture in Settings › Picture helps.

Motion looks choppy

With Headwire Connect, set Optimize for to Responsiveness, and connect by USB cable if you can (How your iPad or iPhone connects). Under Automatic, Low Power Mode uses Battery Life, which sends fewer frames.

Blacks look grey, or colours look off

The capture card labels its picture wrongly. Open its profile and set Read as under Brightness range or Colour (Correct the picture yourself).

Sound comes before or after the picture

In Settings › Sound, move Sound timing towards Later if the sound comes first, or Earlier if it comes after.

A key stays held down on your Mac

In Settings › Keyboard, tap Release all keys.

Command-Tab or Command-Space doesn’t reach your Mac

iPadOS keeps them. Press Option-Tab or Option-Space instead, or add your own in Send the shortcuts iPadOS keeps.

Some keys type the wrong characters

Over Bluetooth, Headwire types through your Mac’s keyboard layout. Set the one your Mac uses in Settings › Keyboard › Mac keyboard layout.

Touch does nothing in a window on iPad

Touch works while Headwire fills the screen. In a window, use Apple Pencil, a mouse or a trackpad, or make Headwire full screen.

Your Mac isn’t in Add Mac

  1. On your Mac, turn on Streaming or Input in the Headwire Connect menu.
  2. Connect your Mac and this iPad or iPhone with a USB cable, or put both on the same network.
  3. If Headwire said Local Network access is off, follow Local Network access is off.

Headwire isn’t in your Mac’s Bluetooth list

  1. In Headwire, open Settings › Add Mac….
  2. Tap Connect over Bluetooth…. This iPad or iPhone gets ready for your Mac to find.
  3. On your Mac, look again in System Settings › Bluetooth (Use your iPad as the Mac’s keyboard and mouse).

The picture shows, but clicks and typing do nothing

Nothing is sending input to your Mac yet; the settings button over the stream carries a small mark.

  • With a capture card only, set up Bluetooth: in Settings, tap Capture card (Use your iPad as the Mac’s keyboard and mouse).
  • With Headwire Connect, on your Mac, turn on Input in the Headwire Connect menu.

The pointer lags, or typing arrives late

  • Connect your Mac and your iPad or iPhone with a USB cable, which has the shortest delay.
  • With Headwire Connect, set Optimize for to Responsiveness in Settings › Picture.
  • On a busy Wi-Fi network, move closer to the router, or use Ethernet on a USB-C adapter.

There’s no sound, and no message

  • In Settings › Sound, check that Play Mac sound on isn’t set to Mac, and that Volume is up.
  • Check this iPad’s or iPhone’s own volume and silent mode.
  • With a capture card, check that Headwire is allowed the Microphone in the Settings app.

The picture

These show on the stream screen, in place of your Mac's picture.

A short video of this is on its way.

No video

No capture card is plugged in and no Headwire Connect link carries your Mac's screen. Choose Connection options to see every way in and what each needs: plug a capture card into your iPad, or open Headwire Connect on your Mac (Start here has both).

Your Mac is asleep or not sending a picture

The capture card is running, but nothing comes from the Mac. Wake your Mac or check its HDMI cable. The picture comes back on its own.

Your Mac’s display is asleep

Headwire Connect is connected, but the Mac's display sleeps. Wake your Mac to see its screen here. The picture comes back on its own.

Connecting to your Mac…

Headwire is starting the stream from Headwire Connect; when the Mac has said its name, the title names it. Nothing to do: the picture follows.

Can’t reach name

With more than one Mac paired, the one you chose can't be reached; the title names it. Make sure it's awake with Headwire Connect open. To use another Mac, choose it in the Macs menu over the stream (Pairing and more than one Mac).

Reconnecting…

The Mac's last frame stays on screen, dimmed, while Headwire waits for a way back to the Mac. It picks up by itself. If it stays, check that your Mac is awake and Headwire Connect is open.

Starting capture…

A capture card is plugged in and Headwire is starting it. If it stays here, unplug the capture card and plug it back in.

Capture paused

The system paused the capture card. The line under the title says why:

  • Headwire went to the background: capture resumes when Headwire is back on screen.
  • Another app is using the capture card: close that app, or stream your Mac's screen with Headwire Connect.
  • Other apps share the screen: capture cards only work while Headwire is the only app on screen.
  • This iPad is too warm: capture resumes when it cools down.
  • Any other reason: it resumes on its own.

Capture card unplugged

Plug the capture card back in to continue.

Capture stopped

Headwire couldn't start the capture card. Unplug it and plug it back in, or choose Try again.

Capture card not ready

The capture card didn't list its video formats in time. Choose Try again. If it keeps happening, unplug the capture card and plug it back in.

Capture card not supported

This capture card doesn't offer a video format Headwire can show. Try a different capture card, or stream with Headwire Connect.

Headwire Connect isn’t streaming

Headwire Connect is connected but may not stream. The line under the title gives the reason, such as a permission to turn on or Streaming switched off. If it says to look in the menu, open the Headwire Connect menu on your Mac (The Headwire Connect menu).

Streaming is off

On your Mac, turn on Streaming in the Headwire Connect menu.

Turn on Screen Recording

  1. On your Mac, open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording.
  2. Turn on Headwire Connect.
  3. If macOS asks, choose Quit & Reopen. The stream starts by itself.

Reopen Headwire Connect

Screen Recording is on, but macOS applies it only after Headwire Connect reopens. On your Mac, quit Headwire Connect and open it again.

Your Mac isn’t sending a picture

Headwire Connect is connected and Streaming is on, but no picture arrived. Headwire asks again by itself; Try again asks now. If it keeps happening, on your Mac, quit Headwire Connect and open it again.

No picture from the Mac

Your Mac's screen gave Headwire Connect no picture when it started; the display may need reconnecting. Headwire Connect tries again by itself, or choose Try again.

Your Mac stopped sharing its screen

macOS stopped the screen capture. Headwire Connect tries again by itself; if the message stays, on your Mac, quit Headwire Connect and open it again.

The display went away

The display Headwire Connect streamed was disconnected. Headwire Connect tries again by itself; if the message stays, on your Mac, quit Headwire Connect and open it again.

The Mac stream stopped

The stream from Headwire Connect ended. Headwire Connect tries again by itself; if the message stays, on your Mac, quit Headwire Connect and open it again.

Your Mac’s video encoder stopped

Headwire Connect stopped streaming because your Mac's video encoder kept stopping. On your Mac, choose Try Again in the Headwire Connect menu.

Your Mac has no display

Headwire Connect from the App Store streams a display the Mac already has.

  • On your Mac, plug in the capture card, a monitor or an HDMI dummy plug.
  • Or install Headwire Connect from the Headwire website, which adds a display by itself.
  • If macOS refused that display, on your Mac, quit Headwire Connect and open it again.

Your Mac shows its own display instead of extending

When you choose Extend and your Mac shows its own display instead, the line under the display setting says why, and what to do:

A short video of this is on its way.
  • Headwire Connect from the App Store can't add a display. On your Mac, install Headwire Connect from the Headwire website.
  • Extending is off on the Mac: on your Mac, turn on Let devices extend this Mac in Headwire Connect, and turn on Streaming.
  • Your Mac can't make a display this shape: rotate the screen to try another shape.
  • Another device is using the extended display: disconnect it to extend to this one.
  • Your Mac couldn't add a display: on your Mac, quit Headwire Connect and open it again.
  • Headwire Connect or macOS is too old: on your Mac, update Headwire Connect.

Sound

Sound problems show in Settings, beside the sound setting.

A short video of this is on its way.

No sound from this capture card

This capture card doesn't carry sound. To hear your Mac here, use one that carries sound, or stream with Headwire Connect.

Several sound inputs

Several sound inputs are plugged in, and none is named like the capture card. Unplug the ones you don't need.

Sound couldn’t start

  1. Close other apps that use sound.
  2. In Settings › Sound, set Play Mac sound on to Mac.
  3. Set it back to this iPad or iPhone.

Sound stopped

The capture card’s sound stopped. Plug the capture card back in.

No sound from your Mac

Headwire Connect isn't sending sound. The line under the title says why.

  1. On your Mac, check that Streaming is on in the Headwire Connect menu.
  2. If the line says this version doesn't send sound, update Headwire Connect.
  3. If the sound stopped, turn Streaming off and on again.

Connecting to Headwire Connect

These show on your Mac’s page in Settings, under Status.

A short video of this is on its way.

Can’t find Headwire Connect

  1. On your Mac, check that Headwire Connect is open.
  2. Check that your Mac and this iPad or iPhone are on the same network. Some hotel and guest Wi-Fi hides devices from each other; connect by USB cable there.
  3. In Headwire, choose Try again.

Your Mac isn’t answering

Your Mac is on this network, but this iPad or iPhone can't connect to it. The Wi-Fi may stop devices reaching each other, or a VPN may be in the way.

  1. Connect your Mac and this iPad or iPhone by USB cable, or join another network.
  2. In Headwire, choose Try again.

Headwire Connect disconnected

Your iPad or iPhone tries again by itself. If it keeps happening, on your Mac, quit Headwire Connect and open it again.

Another user is on your Mac

Headwire Connect pauses while another account is in use on your Mac. On your Mac, switch back to your account; your iPad or iPhone connects by itself.

Streaming and Input are off

On your Mac, turn on Streaming or Input in the Headwire Connect menu. Your iPad or iPhone connects by itself once one is on.

Your Mac is asleep

Headwire Connect pauses while your Mac sleeps. Wake your Mac; your iPad or iPhone connects by itself.

Your Mac is restarting or shutting down

Headwire Connect closed as your Mac logs out, restarts or shuts down. Once your Mac is back, log in; your iPad or iPhone connects by itself when Headwire Connect opens.

Headwire Connect quit

On your Mac, open Headwire Connect again. Your iPad or iPhone connects by itself when it's back.

Not recognised by your Mac

Your Mac no longer recognises this iPad or iPhone, usually because it was forgotten in Headwire Connect. Choose Pair again.

Pair with your Mac again

Headwire Connect on your Mac has a new key, so your iPad or iPhone can't be sure it's the one it paired with. Choose Pair again to keep using it.

Update Headwire Connect

On your Mac, update Headwire Connect: through the App Store for Headwire Connect from the App Store, or with the update rows in its Settings › General for Headwire Connect from the Headwire website. Get Headwire Connect always has the newest.

Update Headwire

Headwire Connect on your Mac is newer than Headwire on this iPad or iPhone. Update Headwire in the App Store. If the message stays, update Headwire Connect too.

Can’t read Headwire’s keys

Headwire couldn't read its pairing keys from the keychain on your iPad or iPhone. Unlock it, then choose Try again.

Local Network access is off

Headwire needs Local Network access to find your Mac over a local network or peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. A USB cable, a capture card and Bluetooth work without it.

  1. Tap Open Settings.
  2. In the Settings app, turn on Local Network.

Bluetooth

With a capture card, Headwire reaches your Mac as a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse (Capture card and Bluetooth).

A short video of this is on its way.

Bluetooth is off

Turn on Bluetooth in Control Center or the Settings app, or connect to Headwire Connect on your Mac.

Bluetooth keyboard didn’t start

  1. In Control Center, turn Bluetooth off and on.
  2. In Headwire, choose Try again.

Or connect through Headwire Connect instead.

Bluetooth keyboard not available

This iPad or iPhone can't be a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse. Connect to Headwire Connect on your Mac instead, by cable or wirelessly.

Bluetooth isn’t taking input

Turn Bluetooth off and on in Control Center, or connect to Headwire Connect on your Mac.

Pairing didn’t finish

Pairing over Bluetooth through Headwire Connect didn't finish. The line under it says why:

  • the codes didn't match;
  • the code wasn't confirmed within a minute;
  • your Mac didn't answer, or couldn't find this iPad or iPhone;
  • Bluetooth is off or not allowed on your Mac;
  • another device was pairing at the same time.

Do what it says, then choose Try again. Tap They match only if both codes are the same.

Pair from System Settings

Headwire Connect can’t pair for you on this Mac.

  1. On your Mac, open System Settings › Bluetooth.
  2. Under Nearby Devices, click Connect beside Headwire.
  3. Check that both screens show the same code, then confirm it on your Mac.
  4. On this iPad or iPhone, tap Pair in the alert.

Permissions

Each of these offers Open Settings, which opens Headwire's page in the Settings app.

A short video of this is on its way.

Camera access is off

Headwire reads capture cards as a camera. Turn on Camera for Headwire, or stream your Mac's screen with Headwire Connect.

Microphone access is off

Headwire can't play the capture card's sound without it. Turn on Microphone for Headwire, or stream your Mac's sound with Headwire Connect. Headwire only takes the capture card's sound, never the built-in microphone.

Bluetooth access is off

Headwire reaches your Mac as a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse. Turn on Bluetooth for Headwire, or connect to Headwire Connect on your Mac.

Photos access is off

Headwire isn't allowed to add screenshots and recordings to your photo library. Turn on Photos for Headwire, or use Share instead.

Blocked by Screen Time or your organisation

Screen Time or a management profile blocks a permission, so there's no switch to offer. The line names what's blocked and the way around it: Headwire Connect streams your Mac's screen and sound and takes input by cable or wirelessly, and Share keeps a screenshot or recording.

Calibration

A short video of this is on its way.

Calibration didn't finish

The line under the title says why. Fix it, then choose Try again (Calibration).

  • Headwire Connect isn’t connected, or can’t show the test pattern: on your Mac, open Headwire Connect and turn on Streaming.
  • It’s showing the pattern for another device: wait for that calibration to end.
  • It can’t find the capture card’s display: choose it under Capture Card in the Headwire Connect menu.
  • The pattern didn’t come through, or it took too long: keep the capture card streaming, and try again.

Tips couldn't load, Tip didn't go through or Past tips couldn't be restored

The line under the title says why:

  • Headwire couldn't reach the App Store: check that this iPad or iPhone is online, then try again.
  • In-app purchases are blocked: allow them in Screen Time in the Settings app, or ask whoever manages this iPad or iPhone.
  • The App Store didn't send the tips, or couldn't finish: try again in a moment.
