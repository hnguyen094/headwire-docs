Troubleshooting

When something stops Headwire, it says so on screen with a title, what to do, and the one button that helps: Open Settings, Try again or Pair again. Each title below is the one Headwire shows, so you can find yours.

Still stuck? Headwire Support has the questions that come up most and how to reach the developer. In Headwire, Settings › About › Send feedback writes an email with the app and system versions filled in.

The picture

These show on the stream screen, in place of your Mac's picture.

A short video of this is on its way.

No video

No capture card is plugged in and no Headwire Connect link carries your Mac's screen. Choose Connection options to see every way in and what each needs: plug a capture card into your iPad, or open Headwire Connect on your Mac (Start here has both).

Your Mac is asleep or not sending a picture

The capture card is running, but nothing comes from the Mac. Wake your Mac or check its HDMI cable. The picture comes back on its own.

Your Mac’s display is asleep

Headwire Connect is connected, but the Mac's display sleeps. Wake your Mac to see its screen here. The picture comes back on its own.

Connecting to your Mac…

Headwire is starting the stream from Headwire Connect; when the Mac has said its name, the title names it. Nothing to do: the picture follows.

Can’t reach your Mac

With more than one Mac paired, the one you chose can't be reached; the title names it. Make sure it's awake with Headwire Connect open. To use another Mac, choose it in the Macs menu over the stream (Pairing and more than one Mac).

Reconnecting…

The Mac's last frame stays on screen, dimmed, while Headwire waits for a way back to the Mac. It picks up by itself.

Starting capture…

A capture card is plugged in and Headwire is starting it. If it stays here, unplug the capture card and plug it back in.

Capture paused

The system paused the capture card. The line under the title says why:

  • Headwire went to the background: capture resumes when Headwire is back on screen.
  • Another app is using the capture card: close that app, or stream your Mac's screen with Headwire Connect.
  • Other apps share the screen: capture cards only work while Headwire is the only app on screen.
  • This iPad is too warm: capture resumes when it cools down.
  • Any other reason: it resumes on its own.

Capture card unplugged

Plug the capture card back in to continue.

Capture stopped

Headwire couldn't start the capture card. Unplug it and plug it back in, or choose Try again.

Capture card not ready

The capture card didn't list its video formats in time. Choose Try again. If it keeps happening, unplug the capture card and plug it back in.

Capture card not supported

This capture card doesn't offer a video format Headwire can show. Try a different capture card, or stream with Headwire Connect.

Headwire Connect isn’t streaming

Headwire Connect is connected but may not stream. The line under the title gives the reason, such as a permission to turn on or Streaming switched off. If it says to look in the menu, open the Headwire Connect menu on your Mac (The Headwire Connect menu).

Streaming is off

On your Mac, turn on Streaming in the Headwire Connect menu.

Turn on Screen Recording

On your Mac, open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording, then turn on Headwire Connect. If macOS asks, choose Quit & Reopen. The stream starts once it's on.

Reopen Headwire Connect

Screen Recording is on, but macOS applies it only after Headwire Connect reopens. On your Mac, quit Headwire Connect and open it again.

Your Mac isn’t sending a picture

Headwire Connect is connected and Streaming is on, but no picture arrived. Headwire asks again by itself; Try again asks now. If it keeps happening, on your Mac, quit Headwire Connect and open it again.

No picture from the Mac

Your Mac's screen gave Headwire Connect no picture when it started; the display may need reconnecting. Headwire Connect tries again by itself, or choose Try again.

Your Mac stopped sharing its screen, The display went away, The Mac stream stopped

macOS stopped the screen capture, the streamed display was disconnected, or the stream ended. Headwire Connect tries again by itself; when it can't, on your Mac, quit Headwire Connect and open it again.

Your Mac’s video encoder stopped

Headwire Connect stopped streaming because your Mac's video encoder kept stopping. On your Mac, choose Try Again in the Headwire Connect menu.

Your Mac has no display

Headwire Connect from the App Store streams a display the Mac already has. On your Mac, plug in the capture card or an HDMI adapter, or use Headwire Connect from the Headwire website, which adds a display by itself. If macOS refused that display, plug one in, or quit Headwire Connect and open it again.

Extending your Mac

When you choose Extend and your Mac shows its own display instead, the line under the display setting says why, and what to do:

A short video of this is on its way.
  • Headwire Connect from the App Store can't add a display. On your Mac, install Headwire Connect from the Headwire website.
  • Extending is off on the Mac: on your Mac, turn on Let devices extend this Mac in Headwire Connect, and turn on Streaming.
  • Your Mac can't make a display this shape: rotate the screen to try another shape.
  • Another device is using the extended display: disconnect it to extend to this one.
  • Your Mac couldn't add a display: on your Mac, quit Headwire Connect and open it again.
  • Headwire Connect or macOS is too old: on your Mac, update Headwire Connect.

Sound

Sound problems show in Settings, beside the sound setting.

A short video of this is on its way.

No sound from this capture card

This capture card doesn't carry sound. To hear your Mac here, use one that carries sound, or stream with Headwire Connect.

Several sound inputs

Several sound inputs are plugged in, and none is named like the capture card. Unplug the ones you don't need.

Sound couldn’t start

Close other apps that use sound, then set Play Mac sound on to Mac and back to this iPad or iPhone.

Sound stopped

The capture card's sound stopped. It plays again when the capture card is plugged back in.

No sound from your Mac

Headwire Connect isn't sending sound. On your Mac, turn on Streaming in Headwire Connect, or update Headwire Connect if the line says this version doesn't send sound. If the Mac's sound stopped, turn Streaming off and on again.

Connecting to Headwire Connect

These show in Connection, where you choose how Headwire reaches your Mac.

A short video of this is on its way.

Can’t find Headwire Connect

On your Mac, check that Headwire Connect is open and that your Mac is on the same network as your iPad or iPhone. Some hotel and guest Wi-Fi hides devices from each other; connect by cable there. Then choose Try again.

Your Mac isn’t answering

Your Mac is on this network, but your iPad or iPhone can't connect to it. If your Mac is awake, the Wi-Fi may be stopping devices reaching each other, or a VPN on either device may be in the way. Connect by cable, or try another network, then choose Try again.

Headwire Connect disconnected

Your iPad or iPhone tries again by itself. If it keeps happening, on your Mac, quit Headwire Connect and open it again.

Headwire Connect is busy

Headwire Connect on your Mac can't take another device right now. Your iPad or iPhone connects by itself when it can.

Another user is on your Mac

Headwire Connect pauses while another account is in use on your Mac, and connects by itself when you switch back.

Streaming and Input are off

On your Mac, turn on Streaming or Input in the Headwire Connect menu. Your iPad or iPhone connects by itself once one is on.

Your Mac is asleep

Headwire Connect pauses while your Mac sleeps, and your iPad or iPhone connects by itself when it wakes.

Your Mac is restarting or shutting down

Headwire Connect closed as your Mac logs out, restarts or shuts down. Your iPad or iPhone connects by itself when it's open again.

Headwire Connect quit

On your Mac, open Headwire Connect again. Your iPad or iPhone connects by itself when it's back.

Not recognised by your Mac

Your Mac no longer recognises this iPad or iPhone, usually because it was forgotten in Headwire Connect. Choose Pair again.

Pair with your Mac again

Headwire Connect on your Mac has a new key, so your iPad or iPhone can't be sure it's the one it paired with. Choose Pair again to keep using it.

Update Headwire Connect

On your Mac, update Headwire Connect to use it with this version of Headwire.

Update Headwire

Headwire Connect on your Mac is newer than Headwire on your iPad or iPhone, or the two can't understand each other. Update Headwire, and if it still shows, update both to the latest version.

Can’t read Headwire’s keys

Headwire couldn't read its pairing keys from the keychain on your iPad or iPhone. Unlock it, then choose Try again.

Local Network access is off

Headwire needs Local Network access to find your Mac over a local network or peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. A USB cable, a capture card and Bluetooth work without it. Choose Open Settings and turn on Local Network.

Bluetooth

With a capture card, Headwire reaches your Mac as a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse (Capture card and Bluetooth).

A short video of this is on its way.

Bluetooth is off

Turn on Bluetooth in Control Center or the Settings app, or connect to Headwire Connect on your Mac.

Bluetooth keyboard didn’t start

Your iPad or iPhone couldn't start its Bluetooth keyboard. Turn Bluetooth off and on in Control Center and choose Try again, or connect to Headwire Connect on your Mac.

Bluetooth keyboard not available

This iPad or iPhone can't be a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse. Connect to Headwire Connect on your Mac instead, by cable or wirelessly.

Bluetooth isn’t taking input

Turn Bluetooth off and on in Control Center, or connect to Headwire Connect on your Mac.

Pairing didn’t finish

Pair over Bluetooth, which Headwire Connect answers for your Mac, didn't finish. The line under it says why: the codes didn't match, the code wasn't confirmed within a minute, your Mac didn't answer, your Mac couldn't find this iPad or iPhone, Bluetooth is off or not allowed on your Mac, or another device was pairing at the same time. Do what it says, then choose Try again. Tap They match only if both codes are the same.

Pair from System Settings

Headwire Connect can't pair for you on this Mac. On your Mac, open System Settings › Bluetooth and connect to Headwire. Both screens show a code; check they match.

Permissions

Each of these offers Open Settings, which opens Headwire's page in the Settings app.

A short video of this is on its way.

Camera access is off

Headwire reads capture cards as a camera. Turn on Camera for Headwire, or stream your Mac's screen with Headwire Connect.

Microphone access is off

Headwire can't play the capture card's sound without it. Turn on Microphone for Headwire, or stream your Mac's sound with Headwire Connect. Headwire only takes the capture card's sound, never the built-in microphone.

Bluetooth access is off

Headwire reaches your Mac as a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse. Turn on Bluetooth for Headwire, or connect to Headwire Connect on your Mac.

Photos access is off

Headwire isn't allowed to add screenshots and recordings to your photo library. Turn on Photos for Headwire, or use Share instead.

Blocked by Screen Time or your organisation

Screen Time or a management profile blocks a permission, so there's no switch to offer. The line names what's blocked and the way around it: Headwire Connect streams your Mac's screen and sound and takes input by cable or wirelessly, and Share keeps a screenshot or recording.

Files

Files go between your iPad or iPhone and your Mac through Headwire Connect.

A short video of this is on its way.
  • Sending files needs Headwire Connect: on your Mac, install or open Headwire Connect, then drop the file again.
  • Too large: Headwire sends and takes files up to 2 GB.
  • Your Mac is out of space, or this iPad or iPhone is: free up space, then send the file again.
  • Your Mac is taking another file: drop it again when that one is done.
  • The connection to your Mac ended: once it's back, send the file again.
  • Your Mac couldn't save the file: on your Mac, check that the Downloads folder is there.
  • Stopped on your Mac: Headwire Connect stopped the file; drop it again to send it.

Calibration

When a capture card calibration can't finish, Headwire says Calibration didn't finish with Try again (Capture card profiles). The line says why: Headwire Connect isn't connected or can't show the test pattern, it is showing the pattern for another device, it can't find the display the capture card is plugged into (choose it under Capture Card in the Headwire Connect menu), the pattern didn't come through, or it took too long.
