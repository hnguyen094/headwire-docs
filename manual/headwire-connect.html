Headwire Connect

The Mac app: your Mac by USB cable or wirelessly, with the most features.

What you need

A short video of this is on its way.
  • A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 27.
  • Headwire Connect from Get Headwire Connect.
  • A USB cable, or the same network.

Headwire Connect from the Headwire website can extend your Mac’s display. Headwire Connect from the App Store shows a display the Mac already has.

Set up and see your Mac

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. On your Mac, click Turn On for Streaming, then allow it in System Settings. If asked, choose Quit & Reopen.
  2. Click Turn On for Input, then allow it in System Settings.
  3. Click Show Pairing Code.
  4. In Headwire, open Settings › Add Mac….
  5. Tap your Mac.
  6. Type the 12 numbers; none over a USB cable. Your Mac appears.
