Capture card and Bluetooth

Nothing to install on your Mac: a capture card for the picture, Bluetooth for control.

Set up a capture card

A short video of this is on its way.

You need:

  • An iPad with USB-C.
  • An “HDMI to USB” capture card.
  • An HDMI cable.
  1. Connect your Mac’s HDMI to the capture card.
  2. Plug the capture card into your iPad.
  3. Open Headwire, then allow Camera, Microphone and Bluetooth.
  4. On your Mac, in System Settings › Bluetooth, click Connect beside Headwire.
  5. Check the codes match, then confirm on your Mac.
  6. On this iPad, tap Pair.
  • Resolution: the capture card’s format page.
  • Sound timing: Sound.

On iPhone, capture cards may not work. Use Headwire Connect.
