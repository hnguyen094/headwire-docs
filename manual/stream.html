The buttons

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. Capture: screenshots and recordings.
  2. Picture in Picture: a small window.
  3. Keyboard: the on-screen keyboard.
  4. What works now: what works on this connection.
  5. Settings: every setting.

With two Macs, Macs joins them. While you use the Mac, they fold into a chevron; tap it to open them.

Fit the picture

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. In Settings › Picture, tap Fit, Fill or Zoom to content.
  • Fit: all of it.
  • Fill: crops the edges.
  • Zoom to content: drops your Mac’s black border.

Take a screenshot

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. Tap Capture.
  2. Choose Take screenshot, then mark up, share or save it.

Record the screen

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. Tap Capture.
  2. Choose Record screen.

To stop, tap the red dot, or press Control-Option-Command-R.

PressTo
Control-Option-Command-STake a screenshot
Control-Option-Command-RRecord, or stop
Control-Option-Command-TLook at text

Copy, paste and send files

A short video of this is on its way.

With Headwire Connect:

  • Copy on your Mac: paste here.
  • Paste on your Mac: Command-V, or Paste to Mac.
  • Drop a file on the stream: it lands in your Mac’s Downloads.
  • Files from your Mac: in Files › Headwire.
