Touch, pointer and keyboard

Touch, Apple Pencil, a trackpad or mouse, the on-screen keyboard, your own keys and shortcuts.

Choose how touch works

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. In Settings › Touch and pointer, choose Point and tap or Trackpad.
  2. Set the Pointer speed.
ToDo this
Right-clickTap with two fingers
ScrollDrag two fingers
ZoomPinch
Mission ControlSwipe up with three fingers
Switch SpacesSwipe sideways with three fingers
Show the desktopSpread three or four fingers

With Three-finger drag on, Mission Control and Spaces take four fingers. Touch works in full screen; in a window, use Apple Pencil, a mouse or a trackpad.

Use three-finger drag and Apple Pencil

A short video of this is on its way.
  • Three-finger drag: drag windows with three fingers, as on a Mac trackpad.
  • Apple Pencil: touch to click, hover to point. With Scribble, write to type.

Set up a trackpad or mouse

A short video of this is on its way.

In Settings › Touch and pointer:

  • Pointer style: the Mac’s pointer, or the iPad’s.
  • Snap to controls, with a Headwire Connect Mac and Pointer style set to iPad: the pointer holds on buttons, the innermost first.
  • A soft tint marks a clickable area too big to highlight.

Type on the on-screen keyboard

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. Modifier keys: tap to hold for the next key, twice to lock.
  2. esc, Tab and the arrows. Swipe the strip for fn, your own keys and Paste to Mac.

Tap Keyboard over the stream to open it. A hardware keyboard types too; Headwire keeps Control-Option-Command shortcuts for itself.

Make your own keys

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. In Settings › Keyboard, tap Custom keys.
  2. Tap Add key.
  3. Choose Shortcut, Text or Sequence.
  4. Tap Record, then press the keys, or type the text.
  5. Tap ✓ to save.

Send the shortcuts iPadOS keeps

A short video of this is on its way.

Option-Tab sends Command-Tab, and Option-Space sends Command-Space. To add your own:

  1. In Settings › Keyboard › Shortcuts, tap +.
  2. Tap Record, then press the keys.
  3. Choose what to Send.
  4. Under Mac Gets, tap Record, then press the Mac’s keys.
  5. Tap Save.
