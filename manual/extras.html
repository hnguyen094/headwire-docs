Picture in Picture, Glance and What works now

Keep your Mac in sight while you use other apps, see it in widgets and the Dynamic Island, and check what works on the connection you're using.

Picture in Picture

Picture in Picture keeps your Mac in a small window over other apps, so you can watch it while you do something else.

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. While your Mac's picture shows, tap Picture in Picture over the stream.
  2. Your Mac moves into a small window. Move it, resize it, or put it aside as you would any video.
  3. To come back, tap Show here in Headwire, or use the window's own button.
  • Input to your Mac is off while it's in the small window. Headwire says Your Mac is in Picture in Picture in the meantime.
  • To open the window whenever you leave Headwire, turn on Start Picture in Picture automatically in Settings › General.
  • The window shows whichever picture is on screen, from the capture card or Headwire Connect, and follows a switch from one to the other.
  • When the system stops the capture card in the background, the window moves to Headwire Connect's stream if there is one. Otherwise it says the capture card paused, and what to do.

Glance

With Glance at your Mac on, Headwire shows a picture of your Mac in its widgets, and on iPhone in the Dynamic Island, while you're in another app.

A short video of this is on its way.
  • Turn it on in Settings › General. It's off to start with.
  • It only runs while something you started keeps Headwire going: your Mac's sound playing, or Picture in Picture open. Nothing else keeps Headwire awake for it.
  • The picture updates when it starts, when you change Macs, and every 15 seconds.
  • Turn on Only while charging to glance only while this iPad or iPhone is charging.
  • The Lock Screen never shows your Mac's picture, and locking this iPad or iPhone takes it away.

Widgets, the Live Activity and Control Center

These come with Headwire whether Glance is on or not:

  • The Your Macs widget, for the Home Screen and the Lock Screen, lists your paired Macs. Tap one to open Headwire on that Mac. With Glance on, its Home Screen sizes show each Mac's newest picture.
  • The Open Mac control, for Control Center, the Lock Screen and the Action button, opens Headwire on the Mac you choose when you add it.
  • While a Mac is on screen, a Live Activity shows its name, how it's connected, whether its picture is showing, and for how long. Tap it to go back to Headwire.

What works now

What works now, over the stream, lists what works on the connection you're using, and what the stream is doing.

A short video of this is on its way.

Its sections are Picture and Sound, Stream, Keyboard, Pointer, Apple Pencil on iPad, and Clipboard and Files. Each row says how that part of Headwire is doing right now:

  • Works, sometimes with what carries it.
  • Limited: it works with a difference, such as Bluetooth typing only the characters your Mac's keyboard layout has.
  • Unavailable: it doesn't work now. The row says why, and what would make it work.

Tap the info button on a row that doesn't fully work for the longer steps. Tap a section's header to open or close it. Headwire remembers which you left open, and opens a section by itself where something isn't working.

The Stream section shows where the picture comes from, its frame rate, and whether it's SDR or HDR, as it happens. With Advanced mode on in Settings › General, it adds the technical readings, such as the connection, the codec, the bitrate, the round trip and the sound's delay, and the other sections add rows about what Headwire reads from your Mac, such as its keyboard layout and shortcuts.

What works now asks your Mac for nothing; it reads what the connection already reports.
