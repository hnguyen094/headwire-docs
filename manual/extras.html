Keep your Mac in view

Keep your Mac in sight in other apps, and see what works now.

Keep your Mac in a small window

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. Tap Picture in Picture. Your Mac moves into a small window.

Input pauses while it’s small. To open it whenever you leave, turn on Start Picture in Picture automatically in General.

See your Mac in widgets

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. In Settings › General, turn on Glance at your Mac.

Widgets, and the Dynamic Island on iPhone, then show your Mac’s picture while its sound or Picture in Picture plays. The picture never shows on the Lock Screen.

These work with or without Glance:

  • Your Macs widget: your Macs, to tap.
  • Open Mac control: opens a Mac from Control Center.
  • Live Activity: the Mac on screen, and how it’s connected.
