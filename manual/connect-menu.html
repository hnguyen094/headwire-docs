The Headwire Connect menu

Headwire Connect’s menu bar menu on your Mac, and its windows.

The menu

A short video of this is on its way.
  1. Streaming: sends the screen and sound. Needs Screen & System Audio Recording.
  2. Input: lets your iPad or iPhone control the Mac. Needs Accessibility.
  3. Devices: each paired iPad and iPhone. Point at one to send a file, rename or forget it.
  4. Pair a Device…: shows a code to scan or type.
  5. Settings…: the Settings window.

With both switches off, Headwire can’t find your Mac. Anything that needs fixing shows at the top, with its button.

Extend with Sidecar

Below Pair a Device…, in Headwire Connect from the Headwire website only: connect an iPad as Apple’s Sidecar.

Capture Card

Below that, when your Mac’s HDMI goes to a capture card, Display Mode sets what your Mac sends it; Match Headwire follows your iPad.

Capture Display picks which display is the capture card.

The Welcome window

A short video of this is on its way.

Opens the first time: set up and see your Mac. To see it again, use Show Welcome Window… in Settings › General.

Once allowed, Streaming and Input are switches that turn each off and on again. Settings… opens Settings.

Settings

A short video of this is on its way.
  • Open at login, Permissions and Advanced mode, in General.
  • Ask on this Mac before pairing, in Devices: off, a device on a USB cable or Bluetooth pairs without asking, even while the Mac is locked.
  • This Mac's key, in Devices with Advanced mode: compare with Keys.
  • Let devices extend this Mac, in Display: only in Headwire Connect from the Headwire website.
  • Restore previous mode on quit, in Display with Advanced mode: puts the capture card’s mode back.
