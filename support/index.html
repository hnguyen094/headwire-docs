Headwire Support

Last updated 2026-11-05

Need a hand? In Headwire, open Settings › About › Send feedback to email the developer, with the app and system versions filled in, or open an issue. The steps below cover the questions that come up most.

Two ways to connect

Headwire works with a capture card and Bluetooth, with nothing installed on your Mac, or with Headwire Connect on your Mac, by cable or wirelessly and with the most features. Use either, or both: Headwire picks the connection and moves to another when one drops.

A capture card (iPad with USB-C)

Connect an HDMI port on your Mac to a USB video capture card, a standard “HDMI to USB” capture card, and plug the capture card into your iPad. Open Headwire and allow Camera and Microphone when asked: the system treats the capture card as a camera, and its sound as a microphone. Nothing is installed on the Mac, and the capture card takes the place of a monitor, so this works with a Mac mini that has none. The picture comes straight from the Mac’s HDMI port, so it shows the login window too.

Bluetooth keyboard and mouse

Open Headwire and allow Bluetooth. On your Mac, open System Settings › Bluetooth, find Headwire under Nearby Devices and click Connect. Check that both screens show the same code, then confirm on both. Nothing is installed on the Mac.

Headwire Connect on the Mac

For the Mac screen and sound over a USB cable or wirelessly, on iPad or iPhone, with no capture card, install Headwire Connect on your Mac. It also adds the most input features, such as trackpad-style scrolling and typing any character. Get Headwire Connect has the steps.

If something doesn’t work

The capture card shows nothing

  • Check that Headwire may use the camera: in the Settings app on your iPad, open Apps › Headwire and turn on Camera.
  • Check that the Mac is awake and sending to the capture card. On your Mac, the capture card is listed in System Settings › Displays.
  • Video an app protects, as many streaming services do, shows as black through a capture card. That’s the protection working; the rest of the screen still shows.

There’s a picture but no sound from the capture card

In the Settings app on your iPad, open Apps › Headwire and turn on Microphone. Headwire only takes the capture card’s HDMI sound, never the built-in microphone.

Your Mac doesn’t find Headwire over Bluetooth

Once paired, your Mac lists your iPad or iPhone under its own name, not as Headwire. If they were paired before, on your Mac open System Settings › Bluetooth, click the info button next to your iPad or iPhone, choose Forget This Device, then connect again.

Headwire Connect streams nothing

  • In the Headwire Connect menu on your Mac, check that Streaming is on, and that Headwire Connect is allowed in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording.
  • Headwire Connect from the Mac App Store needs a display connected to the Mac to stream, such as a monitor, the capture card, or an HDMI dummy plug. On a Mac with no display, Headwire says so.

Headwire can’t find your Mac

  • Connect the two with a USB cable, or put them on the same Wi-Fi or Ethernet network.
  • Check that Headwire may find devices on your local network: in the Settings app, open Apps › Headwire and turn on Local Network.
  • In the Headwire Connect menu on your Mac, check that Streaming or Input is on; Headwire Connect listens only while one is.

Contact

  • In Headwire, Settings › About › Send feedback opens your email app with a message to the developer. Nothing is sent until you send it.
  • Or write to hungnguyen094+headwire@gmail.com from any email app.
  • Or open an issue at github.com/hnguyen094/headwire-docs/issues. Issues there are public, so leave out anything personal.
